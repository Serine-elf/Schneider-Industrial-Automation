# Schneider-Industrial-Automation
Industrial automation internship at Schneider Electric, focused on PLC programming using TIA Portal, Ladder Logic, and GRAFCET for sequential control systems

About my Experience:
As part of my engineering studies in Automatic Control and Systems Engineering, I had the opportunity to complete an internship at Schneider Electric, where I explored the field of industrial automation and discovered how PLC-based systems are used to control industrial processes.
During this experience, I worked with Siemens TIA Portal and learned the fundamentals of PLC programming using Ladder Logic. I also studied GRAFCET and its application in designing and understanding sequential control systems.

Technical Skills:
This experience helped me develop my knowledge of industrial automation, PLC programming, Ladder Logic, GRAFCET, and Siemens TIA Portal. It also strengthened my understanding of control systems and the interaction between hardware and software.
My broader technical interests include Python, C/C++, MATLAB, embedded systems, electronics, robotics, and software development.

About Me:
I am currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Automatic Control and Systems Engineering at USTHB. I enjoy learning, experimenting, and developing technical projects, particularly in automation, embedded systems, robotics, and intelligent technologies.
I believe in continuously improving my skills by combining theoretical knowledge with practical experience.
